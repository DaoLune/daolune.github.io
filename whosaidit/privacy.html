Who Said It? — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026

Who Said It? is a quote guessing game for iPhone and Android, made by Kevin Bosio ("I", "me"). This page explains what data the app uses and why. The short version: the game has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your scores stay on your phone. The only data that leaves your phone goes to Google's ad service (if you see ads) and to Apple or Google (if you buy "Remove ads").

1. Data that stays on your phone

The app saves these on your device only, so the game remembers you between sessions:

  • your scores, best scores, streak and Daily results;
  • which quotes you have found (the Library);
  • your settings: language, sound, chosen categories, daily reminder on or off;
  • whether you bought "Remove ads".

I never receive this data. Deleting the app deletes it.

2. Daily reminder

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app asks your phone's permission to show notifications. The reminder is scheduled on your phone. No server is involved and no notification token is sent to me. You can turn it off in the game's settings or in your phone's settings.

3. Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect data from your device, such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address (which can give an approximate location), device type and operating system, and how you interact with ads.

  • Europe (EEA), UK and Switzerland: the app shows Google's consent form before personalised ads are used. You can choose non-personalised ads.
  • iPhone: the app asks Apple's "Allow tracking?" question. If you say no, the advertising identifier is not shared and ads are not personalised with it.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads).

Google handles this data under its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. "Remove ads" purchase

"Remove ads" is a one-time purchase handled entirely by the App Store (Apple) or Google Play. I do not see your name, email or payment details. The app only learns whether the purchase is active, so it can hide ads and restore the purchase on a new phone. See the Apple Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Sharing a result

When you tap Share, the app opens your phone's own share menu with a picture or text of your result. Nothing is shared unless you choose an app and send it.

6. What the app does not do

  • No account, sign-in, or email address.
  • No analytics or tracking service of my own.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.
  • No selling of personal data.

8. Children

The game is for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. I do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child's data was collected through ads, contact me and I will help you reach Google.

9. Your rights

Because I keep no personal data on any server, there is nothing about you for me to show, correct or delete. For data held by Google or Apple, use their privacy tools linked above. If you live in the EU or UK, you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, I will update this page and its effective date.
